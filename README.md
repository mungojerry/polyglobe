Polyglobe
================

An up to date version of Zarch. Player patrols a small planet searching for the "Seeder" craft that are seeding a virus into the land. Clean the alien surge from the planet! Fighters will try and dissaude you from doing this, so be careful! 

Player must refuel at their bases, and also prevent their bases from becoming part of the infected!

Action takes place on a planet. Players are sent down from a mothership to the planets surface to try and cleanse the planet. Each planet has unique environments, rocky, mountainous, wet, desert etc. with their own hazards



Enemy Types
------------

- Seeders - seed the virus onto the land
- Seekers - seek you out and pester you - trying to collide with you
- Fighters - attack the craft with range missiles
- Dreadnought - Large enemy ship the patrols the skies and dispatches seekers and fighters to attack the player
